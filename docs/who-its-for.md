# Who Latticer Time is for

## The short version

Latticer Time is for **one person who sells their time**: a freelancer, independent consultant or small-practice owner who already keeps client information in Airtable and wants a timer that writes straight into it.

It is built around one need: **being able to say, with numbers, when a client's included hours are used up.**

## You are a good fit if

- You work **alone** (or you are the only person who logs time).
- You sell **retainers or capped blocks of hours**, such as "5 hours a week for 12 weeks," and you need evidence when the hours run out.
- You also want to see **where your unbilled time goes**: sales, marketing, partnerships, building your own tools. You want that in the same place as client time.
- You like **owning your data**. You are comfortable with Airtable, and you can follow a setup guide that involves a Cloudflare account.
- You log time from a **phone as well as a laptop**, and you step away mid-task (a walk, a meal, a call) and need to pause rather than start over.

## What it helps you do

| You want to... | How the tool helps |
| --- | --- |
| Know when a retainer is nearly used up | Per-engagement included hours, with warnings at 75%, 90% and 100% |
| Keep a weekly pace | Weekly and monthly caps with "near cap" and "cap reached" flags |
| Bill or report fairly | Per-entry rounding you choose per engagement (for example nearest 15 minutes for client work, exact for your own) |
| Track work you do not bill | Internal projects use the same structure as client work, so one report covers everything |
| Attribute unbilled work to a customer | An optional "for customer" tag on internal entries (an addendum you write for a client, a proposal you prepare) |
| Stop for a break without losing context | Pause and resume the running timer; your note and selections stay put |
| Send a clean monthly summary | An optional script turns a month of entries into a one-page Word statement |

## You should use something else if

- You need **teams, permissions or multiple people's timesheets.** This is a single-user tool.
- You need **invoicing, payments or hourly rates** built in. It counts hours; it does not bill.
- You want a **live client dashboard.** By design, clients get a reviewed monthly statement instead. [Why](why-it-works-this-way.md#why-clients-get-a-statement-not-a-login).
- You do not want to use Airtable. The data layer is small and could be swapped, but nothing else is provided.

## What you need before you start

- An Airtable account (check that your plan supports rollup and formula fields).
- A Cloudflare account (the free tier covers one person).
- About an hour for the first setup. Daily use takes seconds.
